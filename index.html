A few things you might be wondering

Let’s clear things up.

What actually works in this version?

Microphone recording, audio playback, timestamped notes, image slides, PDF viewing, slide markers, local session saving, and downloading notes and recordings. There are no accounts, cloud sync, live support or AI transcripts.

Where are my recordings stored?

In this browser on this device, using IndexedDB when you click Save. They are not uploaded. Clearing site data, using a different browser, or changing the site address can make sessions unavailable. Keep downloaded backups.

Can it record my lecturer or an online class?

It records your microphone, not your computer’s internal audio. Check your lecturer’s recording rules and permission before recording. For online classes, headphones prevent the microphone from picking up the class audio.

Do my slides sync automatically?

Image slide changes are timestamped while you record. For PDFs, change pages inside the PDF viewer and click Mark this slide. During review, clicking a saved image-slide marker restores that slide and seeks the recording.

Why won’t my microphone start?

Open the site through Live Server at localhost or 127.0.0.1, allow microphone access, and use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser. A deployed version needs HTTPS. Check your operating system’s microphone permissions too.
